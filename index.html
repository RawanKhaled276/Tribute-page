Marie Curie

The first woman to win a Nobel Prize

Here's a time line of Marie Curie

  • 1867 – Born in Warsaw, Poland
  • 1898 – Discovered polonium and radium with Pierre Curie
  • 1903 – Won Nobel Prize in Physics
  • 1911 – Won Nobel Prize in Chemistry
  • 1934 – Died from aplastic anemia due to radiation exposure
